Built-in AI

An advisor that shows its work.

Ask about your shortlist the way you'd ask a counselor. The AI answers from real data and your own priorities, and tells you where each fact came from.

  • Ask in plain language

    “Is Yale a reach for me?” No filters or spreadsheets needed.

    Answers cite the College Scorecard fields they used, so you can check every number.
